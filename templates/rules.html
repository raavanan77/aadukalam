Game Rules ⚽

Objective

Pick one team from each group (A–L) before the tournament starts. Your selected teams will earn points based on their real match performance.

Group Stage Scoring

During the group stage, your selected teams will earn:

  • Win → +2 points
  • Draw → +1 points
  • Loss → 0 points

Knockout Stage Scoring

Once your team qualifies for playoffs:

  • Win in knockout → +5 points

Final Match Bonus

If your team wins the FINAL:

  • Final Victory → +10 points

Important Rules

  • You can pick only one team per group
  • Picks are locked before tournament starts
  • Points are calculated automatically after match results are updated
  • Leaderboard ranks players based on total points

Winning

The player with the highest total points at the end of the tournament wins.
